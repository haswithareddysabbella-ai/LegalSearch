# LegalSearch ⚖️

LegalSearch is an intelligent legal research and document analysis platform designed to help legal professionals, researchers, and students quickly search, analyze, and extract insights from legal texts, case laws, and statutory documents.

---

## Features

- **Advanced Document Search:** Quickly find relevant clauses, case precedents, and acts using keyword or semantic search.
- **AI-Powered Summarization:** Generate concise summaries of lengthy legal judgments.
- **Citation & Reference Tracking:** Easily locate and cross-reference legal citations.
- **Secure Document Management:** Upload, organize, and query your own private repository of legal documents.
